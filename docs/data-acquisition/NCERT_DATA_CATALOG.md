# NCERT Data Catalog & Syllabus Mapping

## 1. Overview & Pedagogical Purpose

NCERT textbooks (Classes 6 through 12) form the fundamental knowledge backbone of the UPSC Civil Services Examination. Direct conceptual questions in Prelims GS1 and analytical foundations in Mains GS1–GS3 are traceable directly to specific NCERT chapters.

## 2. Textbooks Catalog Structure

### A. Indian Polity & Governance
1. **Class 11**: *Indian Constitution at Work*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?keps2=0-10`
   - Key Chapters: Constitution: Why and How?, Rights in the Indian Constitution, Election and Representation, Executive, Legislature, Judiciary, Federalism, Local Governments.
   - Linked PYQs: CSE 2024 (Fundamental Rights, Writ Jurisdiction), CSE 2023 (Due Process vs Procedure Established by Law).

2. **Class 11**: *Political Theory*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?keps1=0-8`
   - Key Chapters: Political Theory: An Introduction, Freedom, Equality, Social Justice, Rights, Citizenship, Nationalism, Secularism.
   - Linked PYQs: CSE 2021 (Definition of State), CSE 2020 (Liberty and Law).

### B. Indian & World Geography
3. **Class 11**: *India: Physical Environment*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?kegy1=0-7`
   - Key Chapters: India — Location, Structure and Physiography, Drainage System, Climate, Natural Vegetation, Soils.
   - Linked PYQs: CSE 2024 (Monsoon Circulations, Western Ghats rivers), CSE 2022 (Soil Salinization, Black Soils).

4. **Class 11**: *Fundamentals of Physical Geography*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?kegy2=0-14`
   - Key Chapters: Origin and Evolution of the Earth, Interior of the Earth, Distribution of Oceans and Continents, Geomorphic Processes, Atmosphere Composition and Structure, Solar Radiation, Heat Balance and Temperature, Atmospheric Circulation and Weather Systems, Water in the Atmosphere, World Climate and Climate Change, Water (Oceans), Movements of Ocean Water, Life on the Earth, Biodiversity and Conservation.

### C. Indian Economy & Development
5. **Class 11**: *Indian Economic Development*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?keec1=0-10`
   - Key Chapters: Indian Economy on the Eve of Independence, Indian Economy (1950–1990), Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation: An Appraisal, Poverty, Human Capital Formation in India, Rural Development, Employment: Growth, Informalisation and other issues, Infrastructure, Environment and Sustainable Development, Comparative Development Experiences of India and Its Neighbours.

6. **Class 12**: *Introductory Macroeconomics*
   - Official Portal: `https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php?leec1=0-6`
   - Key Chapters: Introduction to Macroeconomics, National Income Accounting, Money and Banking, Determination of Income and Employment, Government Budget and the Economy, Open Economy Macroeconomics.
   - Linked PYQs: CSE 2024 (CBDC Balance Sheet Liability), CSE 2023 (Sterilization by RBI), CSE 2021 (Money Multiplier).

### D. History & Culture
7. **Class 12**: *Themes in Indian History — Part I*
   - Key Chapters: Bricks, Beads and Bones (Harappan Civilisation), Kings, Farmers and Towns (Early States and Economies), Kinship, Caste and Class, Thinkers, Beliefs and Buildings (Cultural Developments).
8. **Class 12**: *Themes in Indian History — Part II*
   - Key Chapters: Through the Eyes of Travellers, Bhakti-Sufi Traditions, An Imperial Capital: Vijayanagara, Peasants, Zamindars and the State.
9. **Class 12**: *Themes in Indian History — Part III*
   - Key Chapters: Colonialism and the Countryside, Rebels and the Raj (1857 Revolt), Mahatma Gandhi and the Nationalist Movement, Framing the Constitution.

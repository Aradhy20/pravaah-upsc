# Copyright, Legal Compliance & Source Attribution Policy

## 1. Statutory Context & Legal Standards

Pravaah UPSC strictly operates within the legal framework defined by:
- **The Copyright Act, 1957 (India)**, Section 52(1)(a) & 52(1)(h) (Fair dealing for research, private study, review, and publication of questions set in examination).
- **NCERT Terms of Use & Commercial Restrictions**: Prohibits reproduction, mirroring, or commercial sale of complete textbooks and digital PDF assets.
- **Government of India Open Data License**: Allows educational use of gazetted publications, notifications, and parliamentary archives with proper attribution.

---

## 2. Policy by Source Category

### A. Official UPSC Previous Question Papers
- **Status**: Public Domain Examination Record.
- **Attribution**: "Question stems, official answer keys, and option choices are published by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), Dholpur House, New Delhi. Available in original format at https://upsc.gov.in."
- **Commercial Boundary**: Pravaah does not charge for access to public question text. Subscriptions cover cognitive analytics, SuperMemo-2 spaced repetition tracking, interactive exam simulation, and AI Mains grading.

### B. NCERT Textbooks & Curriculum
- **Status**: Protected Intellectual Property of NCERT, New Delhi.
- **Strict Compliance Protocols**:
  1. **Zero PDF Re-hosting**: Pravaah never stores, mirrors, or sells raw NCERT textbook PDFs on its servers or CDNs.
  2. **Canonical Deep Linking**: Every chapter in the NCERT catalog provides an official link directing students to `ncert.nic.in` or `epathshala.nic.in`.
  3. **Original Derivative Summaries**: Conceptual summaries and bullet points are independently authored by Pravaah subject matter experts.

### C. Press Information Bureau (PIB)
- **Status**: Open Government Information.
- **Attribution**: "Official press releases sourced from Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India."

## 1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

Civil services preparation in India has historically been characterized by either exorbitant coaching institutes (charging ₹1.5 to ₹2.5 lakh for broad lectures) or disorganized free PDF dumps that lack accuracy, pedagogic context, and cognitive feedback.

Pravaah UPSC positions itself uniquely in the EdTech landscape through a **Freemium + Recurring Subscription + Specialized Learning Packs** architecture. Rather than charging for raw question catalogs (which are government public domain works under fair dealing), Pravaah monetizes:
1. **Cognitive Time-Savings:** High-density, clutter-free preparation answering "What to study today?".
2. **Pedagogical Intelligence:** AI Personal Mentor grounded in NCERT & standard references.
3. **Examination Simulation:** Authentic UPSC scoring (+2.0 / -0.666 & +2.5 / -0.833) with time-curve telemetry.
4. **Error Elimination:** Spaced repetition and cognitive trap diagnostics (Mistake Intelligence Lab).

## 2. Target Customer Segments

The pricing and product packaging are engineered for eight distinct aspirant personas:

1. **UPSC Beginners (Foundation Stage):**
   - Need syllabus orientation, basic micro-node tracking, and verified PYQs.
   - Served primarily by the Free Aspirant tier.
2. **Serious Prelims Aspirants (Final 6 Months):**
   - Need timed mock simulations, negative marking reduction, and rapid error remediation.
   - Primary target for **Aspirant Pro (₹299/mo · ₹1,999/yr)**.
3. **Mains-Focused Aspirants:**
   - Need structured answer evaluation against UPSC rubrics (Demand, Content, Structure, Presentation).
   - Served by Aspirant Pro/Elite or standalone Mains Evaluation Packs.
4. **Working Professionals:**
   - Constrained by 2–3 hours of daily study time; cannot waste time searching through scattered notes.
   - Benefit immensely from Mission Control daily targets and the AI Mentor.
5. **Hindi-Medium Aspirants:**
   - Suffer from poor automated machine translations on other platforms.
   - Benefit from Pravaah's 100% human-verified Devanagari Hindi parity.
6. **English-Medium Aspirants:**
   - Benefit from precise constitutional citations and Supreme Court case law references.
7. **Repeat-Attempt Candidates (Previous Failures in CSAT / Prelims):**
   - Primary consumers of the CSAT Survival Coach and Mistake Intelligence Lab.
8. **Interview-Stage Candidates:**
   - Target for the Interview & Personality Test Studio and DAF thematic analysis.

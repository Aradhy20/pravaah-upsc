# PRAVAAH UPSC — CURRENT AFFAIRS TO EXAM CONVERTER

**Route:** `/current-affairs/[slug]`  
**API Endpoint:** `/api/v1/current-affairs`  
**Platform:** Pravaah UPSC (`https://pravaah-upsc.vercel.app/`)  
**Core Mission:** Converting Daily News into Actionable UPSC Prelims & Mains Value

---

## 1. Problem Statement: News Overload vs Exam Relevance

Civil services aspirants spend an average of 2 to 3 hours daily consuming newspapers (The Hindu, The Indian Express) and press releases (PIB). However, 85% of standard news reporting (political bickering, sensational crimes, sports results) holds zero exam relevance.

Even when relevant news occurs (e.g., a Supreme Court ruling on bail provisions), aspirants struggle to connect the news story to:
1. Static syllabus topics (e.g., CrPC Section 436A, Article 21, Justice V.S. Malimath Committee).
2. Historical UPSC PYQs testing identical principles.

The **Current Affairs to Exam Converter** systematically transforms daily news developments into structured, high-yield UPSC exam modules.

## 3. Trusted Primary Source Provenance

Pravaah UPSC strictly filters news inputs from verified government and canonical publications:

1. **Press Information Bureau (PIB):** Releases from the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA), PMO, and Ministry of Finance.
2. **The Gazette of India:** Official notifications of enacted Acts, Rules, and Constitutional Amendments.
3. **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Bulletins:** Monetary policy decisions, Financial Stability Report.
4. **Supreme Court Judgments (e-SCR):** Full bench rulings on fundamental rights and basic structure.
5. **NITI Aayog Strategy Reports & Annual Publications:** SDG India Index, Multidimensional Poverty Index.

## 3. CONTENT VERIFICATION & ZERO-HALLUCINATION RULES

1. **Official Final Keys Supersede Provisional Keys:**
   - Any question cancelled or awarded bonus marks by the Commission (e.g. Dropped / Withdrawn questions in official UPSC notices) is explicitly flagged with `DROPPED_BY_UPSC`.
2. **Third-Party Intellectual Property Protection:**
   - Explanations authored by Pravaah faculty are strictly original pedagogical explanations citing statutory acts, Constitutional Articles, Supreme Court judgments, and NCERT textbook sections.
   - We do not reproduce proprietary third-party notes from IASBoard, VisionIAS, or ForumIAS without authorization.
3. **Hindi Paper Authenticity:**
   - Where official bilingual UPSC question papers exist, the Hindi text is extracted verbatim from the Devanagari section of the original examination booklet.
   - For historical questions where only English was digitally archived, translations are generated through academic review and marked with `translationStatus: "HUMAN_REVIEWED"`.

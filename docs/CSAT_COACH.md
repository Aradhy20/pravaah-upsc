Since 2015, CSAT has been a qualifying paper requiring **33% (66.67 marks out of 200)**. However, between 2021 and 2024, the difficulty of CSAT escalated dramatically, with advanced number theory, complex permutations, and lengthy multi-layered reading comprehension passages.

### Pillar 1: Reading Comprehension Mastery
- **The UPSC Linguistic Code:**
  - *"Crux / Best reflects the message"*: Identify the central thesis without extrapolating beyond the text.
  - *"Critical Assumption"*: An unstated premise without which the author's argument collapses.
  - *"Inference"*: A logical consequence that MUST be true based solely on the provided premises.
  - *"Corollary"*: A secondary deduction that flows naturally from the conclusion.

### Pillar 2: High-Yield Quantitative Aptitude
- **Focus on the 80/20 Rule:**
  - 60% of UPSC math questions originate from Number Systems, Divisibility Rules, Unit Digits, and Basic Equations.
  - Advanced calculus, trigonometry, or coordinate geometry are non-existent.
  - Focus on systematic enumeration and algebraic simplification rather than rote formulas.

### Pillar 3: Analytical & Logical Reasoning
- Visual diagramming for syllogisms (Euler/Venn methods).
- Constraint matrices for complex seating arrangements and ranking problems.
- Logical elimination strategies when given contradictory conditional statements.

# Pravaah UPSC — UI/UX Academic Transformation Report
**Date:** 28 November 2026  
**Target Release:** 1 December 2026 (Controlled Beta Launch)  
**Design Standard:** Calm Scholarly Dignity, Devanagari Readability, Cognitive Load Reduction  

---

## 1. Aesthetic Philosophy & Palette

Pravaah UPSC is engineered as an intensive, distraction-free study tool for serious Civil Services aspirants, rejecting the casino-like gamification patterns and garish neon accents common in consumer apps.

| Design Token | Color Value | Purpose & Psychological Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Canvas / Background** | `#FBF7F0` / `stone-50` | Warm alabaster / archival book paper; eliminates harsh screen glare during multi-hour study sessions. |
| **Primary Typography** | `#261B1E` / `stone-900` | Deep archival charcoal; achieves optimal 14:1 contrast ratio against the warm canvas. |
| **Secondary Metadata** | `#786B68` / `stone-600` | Muted stone for tags, years, and booklet codes; reduces visual clutter. |
| **Primary Academic Accent** | `#741A2B` / Deep Burgundy | Prestigious academic burgundy reflecting institutional authority. |
| **Secondary Trust Accent** | `#0F766E` / Deep Teal | Calming focal point for success states, mastery resolution, and verified keys. |
| **Heritage Border** | `#E7DDD4` / `stone-200` | Soft parchment dividers creating clear tactile content boundaries. |

---

## 2. Honest Metrics & Truthful Communication

1. **Elimination of Fabricated Counters:**
   - Previous generic promotional counters ("Over 9,500+ PYQs") were completely eliminated.
   - Replaced with dynamic, database-grounded counters: **2,095+ Verified Authentic PYQs**, **235 NCERT Curriculum Links**, **Series A Booklet Provenance**.

2. **Controlled Beta Transparency:**
   - Hero banner prominently displays: `Controlled Beta · 100% Authentic UPSC CSE Source-Backed Platform`.
   - Clear disclosure that payments are safely paused during beta evaluation.

3. **Academic Errata Mechanism:**
   - Integrated a direct "Report Academic Errata" button in the footer and on every `QuestionCard` to empower aspirants and faculty to report ambiguities or errata.

---

## 3. Bilingual Devanagari Optimization

- **Line Height Safeguards:** All Devanagari stems and options apply `leading-relaxed` and `font-hindi` to prevent clipping of upper (e.g., matras, anusvara) and lower diacritics.
- **Honest Translation Status:** When an authentic UPSC Hindi translation is awaiting gazette digitization, the card explicitly displays a "Translation Pending" notice rather than presenting unreviewed machine translation as official UPSC Hindi text.
- **Synchronized Options:** 1:1 parity between English and Hindi options ensures seamless bilingual switching during practice.

## 1. Statutory Foundation & Constitutional Context

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) is a constitutional body established under Article 315 of the Constitution of India. It administers the Civil Services Examination pursuant to Article 320 to select officers for All-India Services and Central Civil Services.

This document sets forth the legal analysis, intellectual property boundaries, and compliance framework governing the archiving, indexing, and pedagogical utilization of official examination papers on Pravaah UPSC.

## 2. Fair Dealing under Section 52 of the Copyright Act, 1957

Under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the reproduction of government examination questions for instructional, educational, and testing purposes is protected under statutory fair dealing provisions:

### Section 52(1)(h)
> *"The reproduction of any work—*  
> *(i) by a teacher or a pupil in the course of instruction; or*  
> *(ii) as part of the questions to be set in an examination; or*  
> *(iii) in answers to such questions;"*  
> **does not constitute an infringement of copyright.**

### Section 52(1)(a)
> *"A fair dealing with any work, not being a computer program, for the purposes of—*  
> *(i) private or personal use, including research;*  
> *(ii) criticism or review, whether of that work or of any other work..."*

### Application to Pravaah UPSC:
- **Instructional Context:** The platform functions as an online learning and self-assessment environment. Questions are presented to enable aspirants to test their conceptual clarity and prepare for the Civil Services Examination.
- **Transformative Pedagogical Value:** Pravaah UPSC does not merely mirror raw question papers; it enriches each question with comprehensive explanations, NCERT source citations, cognitive error classification, and spaced-repetition analytics.
- **Public Domain Character of Public Service Exams:** Official public examination papers set by constitutional recruitment agencies are matters of public record aimed at selecting civil servants.

## 3. Provenance and Attribution Standards

To maintain total transparency and intellectual honesty:

1. **Explicit Attribution:**
   - Every question record clearly cites the primary examining authority: *"Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) — Civil Services Examination (Preliminary/Main)"*.
   - The original examination year, booklet series, and question number are displayed alongside the question text.

2. **Official Answer Key Integrity:**
   - The platform never claims authorship over the questions or official answer keys.
   - The proprietary creative works of Pravaah UPSC are the **pedagogical explanations, model answers, mind maps, cognitive classification tags, and algorithmic spaced repetition schedules**.

3. **No Commercial Resale of Scanned PDFs:**
   - Pravaah UPSC does not sell raw UPSC question PDFs as independent copyrighted commodities. Access to historical practice questions is integrated into the educational platform.

## 3. Data Integrity & Defect Observations

1. **Test Artifacts in Database:**
   - Two records (`Q9052` and `Q9536`) with text *"With reference to Indian history, who founded the Servants of India Society?"* were generated by the integration test suite (`tests/security_auth_isolation.test.mjs`). Both remain safely in `NEEDS_REVIEW` and are hidden from student-facing views.
2. **CSAT Reading Comprehension Gap:**
   - No passage-based reading comprehension questions currently exist in the database. Historical CSAT papers allocate approximately 25 to 30 questions out of 80 to Reading Comprehension. The ingestion pipeline must preserve passage text and associated question blocks.
3. **Multi-Statement Question Formatting:**
   - UPSC questions frequently use "Consider the following statements (1, 2, 3)" followed by "Which of the statements given above is/are correct?". The database schema stores this cleanly in `questionText` using newline formatting, which the frontend renders faithfully.

## 3. Transparent Renewal & Tax Policy

1. **GST Inclusivity:** All displayed prices on Pravaah UPSC are fully inclusive of 18% Goods & Services Tax (GST). Aspirants never encounter hidden checkout fees.
2. **Renewal Transparency:** Aspirants can toggle auto-renewal off with one click from `/dashboard/billing`. Access remains uninterrupted until the end of the prepaid cycle.
3. **7-Day Money-Back Guarantee:** An unconditional full refund is honored for any quarterly or annual subscription within 7 days of activation.

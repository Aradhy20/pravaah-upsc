# Academic Integrity Rule

1. **UPSC CSE Official Authority**:
   - All Prelims multiple choice questions must be anchored in official UPSC CSE question papers (2013-2024).
   - Correct answers must match official UPSC answer keys without exception. Any disputed or dropped question must be annotated with UPSC's official resolution.

2. **No Fabricated Content**:
   - Never invent mock questions claiming to be official PYQs.
   - Never fabricate question years, booklet series numbers, or marks distributions.

3. **Academic Reference Rigor**:
   - Explanations must cite authoritative textbooks and government documents (NCERT, Constitution of India, PIB, PRS Legislative Research, Economic Survey).
   - Speculative commentary or subjective opinions must never be presented as factual answers.

# Design System Rule

1. **Visual Tone & Hierarchy**:
   - Maintain a calm, scholarly aesthetic suitable for Civil Services examination preparation.
   - Canvas: Warm alabaster / off-white (`#fbfbf9`, `stone-50`).
   - Typography: Crisp dark charcoal (`#1c1917`, `stone-900`) for primary text; muted stone (`#78716c`, `stone-500`) for metadata.
   - Accent: Deep teal (`#0f766e`, `teal-700`) and deep navy (`#1e3a8a`, `blue-900`) for focal points.
   - Avoid garish animations, high-contrast neon borders, or aggressive gamification widgets.

2. **Accessibility & Responsive Layout**:
   - Maintain WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios (4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text).
   - Ensure clean responsive behavior across desktop (1440px), tablet (768px), and mobile (375px).
   - Optimize Devanagari line height (`leading-relaxed`) to prevent clipping of upper and lower matras.

3. **Truthful UI Elements**:
   - All stat counters, progress bars, and inventory numbers must reflect actual database records.
   - Clearly display "Controlled Beta" and honest catalog sizes.

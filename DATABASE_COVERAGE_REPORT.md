# Pravaah UPSC: Historical Database Coverage & Harvest Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Metrics

As of November 2026, the Pravaah UPSC centralized knowledge platform tracks and indexes historical examination papers across **14 major Central Examination Families** spanning **32 historical years (1995–2026)**.

| Metric | Verified Count | Primary Source / Scope |

| **Historical Harvest Tracking Targets (`ExamYear`)** | **448** | Complete examination-year cycles (14 families $\times$ 32 years) |

| **Verified Answer Key Records (`QuestionAnswer`)** | **1,123** | 100% bound to UPSC Official Final Keys & Faculty Keys |
| **Official Source Citations (`SourceCitation`)** | **1,123** | Indexed against UPSC Official Gazette & Master Question Papers |
| **Registered External Knowledge Sources (`KnowledgeSource`)** | **74** | Official portals, UPSC archives, NCERT ePathshala, and secondary providers |
| **Micro-Syllabus Pedagogical Nodes (`SyllabusNode`)** | **53** | Complete micro-topic syllabus mapping across Prelims and Mains |
| **NCERT Books Cataloged (`NCERTBook`)** | **45** | Classes 6–12 History, Geography, Polity, Economy & Science |

## 3. Completeness, Provenance & Gaps

1. **Digital Availability Truth**:
   The Union Public Service Commission maintains verified digital question papers on `upsc.gov.in` primarily from 2014 onwards, with older archives (pre-2014) available in physical gazettes or partially scanned archives.
2. **Explicit Gap Management**:
   Years where examinations were not conducted or archives are unreleased by the government are explicitly flagged as `UNAVAILABLE` or `NOT_HARVESTED`.
3. **Anti-Hallucination Compliance**:
   No synthetic questions or answers have been created to fill missing historical years.
